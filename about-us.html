About ODC Travel Vietnam

Our Story & Heritage

Established in 1997 in Hanoi, ODC Travel (Original Destination Connection) has grown from a passionate local tour provider into one of Vietnam's most reliable travel management agencies. With over 25 years of hands-on industry experience, we specialize in luxury train travel, custom Indochina packages, Halong Bay cruises, and authentic cultural journeys.

Why Travel With Us?

We believe travel should be about meaningful connections rather than standard sightseeing. Our direct partnerships with luxury train operators, boutique hotels, and local guides allow us to deliver exceptional service at net competitive rates without compromising on quality or safety.
