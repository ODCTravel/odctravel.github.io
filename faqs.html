💰 Money & Currency

Is it better to use USD or VND?
While USD is widely accepted in hotels and tours, Vietnam Dong (VND) is best for daily expenses like local food, street stalls, and small purchases. We recommend carrying a mix of both. Make sure your VND notes are not badly torn, as many places may refuse them.

Where can I change money?
You can exchange currency at authorized banks or large hotels. If you are heading to remote areas, please stock up on enough cash (VND) beforehand.

🍜 Travel Experience

Any recommendations for eating in Vietnam?
Try small local restaurants to feel the authentic culture! Stick to busy places with fresh, thoroughly cooked food. Seeing your food cooked (like a steaming bowl of Pho on the street) is often a great bet.

How safe is Vietnam?
Vietnam is among the safest countries to visit. However, like any tourist destination, please be vigilant with your valuables (expensive watches, jewelry) in crowded areas to avoid pickpockets.

When is the best time to visit?
Climate varies by region. Generally, Spring and Autumn are ideal. Tell us your interests, and we will advise the best schedule for you!
